Cosmetic Manager

Upload new cosmetics directly to the live game catalog. No game republish required.
Upload transparent PNG files at native game sizes or 10× artwork, or Photopea-compatible SVG files with embedded PNG artwork. SVG scripts, external images, and unsafe content are blocked. Skin files: head 46×46 or 460×460, hand 20×20 or 200×200, foot 28×14 or 280×140. Pickaxe: 90×60 or 900×600. Shoes: 28×14 or 280×140. Emote: 128×128 or 1280×1280.
